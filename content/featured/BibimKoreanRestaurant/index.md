Collaborated on a multi-role team to design and build a website for Bibim's, a local restaurant. As Project Manager, I coordinated timelines, task ownership, and communication across the team to keep the project on track. As UX Researcher, I gathered and analyzed user needs to inform the site's structure and design decisions, ensuring the final product served both the restaurant's goals and its customers' expectations. As Web Developer, I contributed to building out the site itself, translating research and design decisions into functional, responsive pages.
